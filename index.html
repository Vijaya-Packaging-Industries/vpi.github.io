About Vijaya Packaging

Our Story: A Legacy of Innovation & Trust

Founded in 2019 by visionary entrepreneur Mrs. Vijayalakshmi D, Vijaya Packaging Industries has rapidly evolved from a dedicated corrugated box manufacturing unit into one of the region\'s leading and most trusted packaging solution providers. We are a proud woman-led enterprise, committed to excellence.

Our commitment to unparalleled quality, continuous innovation, and unwavering sustainability has earned us the trust of a diverse clientele across industries including FMCG, electronics, pharmaceuticals, and e-commerce.

Meet Our Founder: Mrs. Vijayalakshmi D

Mrs. Vijayalakshmi\'s journey in the dynamic packaging industry is a testament to her pioneering spirit. She embarked on this path with a clear vision: to create superior, sustainable packaging solutions while fostering economic empowerment within the local community. Her leadership and business acumen have been recognized with several prestigious entrepreneurship awards, including the notable Business Excellence Award in 2018.

Under her inspiring guidance, Vijaya Packaging Industries consistently pushes the boundaries of innovation, all while maintaining an unwavering focus on environmental responsibility and positive social impact.

"We don't just make boxes; we build trust and deliver solutions that protect what's valuable, both for our clients and our planet." - Mrs. Vijayalakshmi D

Our Commitment to Sustainability

Eco-Friendly By Design, Ethical By Choice

At Vijaya Packaging Industries, environmental responsibility is not an afterthought—it\'s integral to our identity. We champion the belief that premium packaging and profound sustainability can, and must, coexist.

Our holistic approach to sustainability is woven into the fabric of our operations, from responsibly sourced raw materials and eco-conscious manufacturing processes to ensuring the end-of-life recyclability of our products.

♻️

Recycled Materials Focus

Our corrugated board proudly contains a high percentage (up to 70-100% where feasible) of post-consumer recycled content, significantly reducing reliance on virgin resources.

💧

Water-Based Inks Exclusively

We exclusively utilize advanced water-based inks, which are free from harmful Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) and heavy metals, ensuring safety and minimal environmental impact.

⚡

Energy Efficiency & Green Practices

Our state-of-the-art facility operates with energy-efficient machinery and embraces green manufacturing practices to actively minimize our carbon footprint.

🌱

Waste Reduction & Circularity

Our robust zero-waste initiative ensures that over 95% of our production waste is meticulously recycled or repurposed, contributing to a circular economy.
