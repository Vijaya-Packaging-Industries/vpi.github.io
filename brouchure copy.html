About Vijaya Packaging

Our Story: Innovation & Trust

Founded in 2019 by visionary entrepreneur Mrs. Vijayalakshmi D, Vijaya Packaging Industries has swiftly grown into a leading and trusted packaging solution provider. As a proud woman-led enterprise, we are deeply committed to excellence in everything we do.

Our dedication to superior quality, ongoing innovation, and strong sustainability practices has built trust with clients across diverse sectors like FMCG, electronics, pharmaceuticals, e-commerce etc,.

Meet Our Founder

Mrs. Vijayalakshmi D is a pioneer in the packaging industry. Her vision extends beyond creating top-tier, sustainable packaging; she aims to foster economic empowerment within our community. Her leadership has been celebrated with prestigious awards.
