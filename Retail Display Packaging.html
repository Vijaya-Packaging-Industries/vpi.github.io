Retail Display Packaging

Capture attention and drive sales with our innovative and customizable retail display packaging solutions.

Our retail display packaging is designed to make your products stand out in a crowded marketplace. From point-of-purchase (POP) displays to shelf-ready packaging (SRP), we create solutions that are not only visually appealing but also functional and easy to assemble.

Types of Retail Displays

Counter Display Units (CDU)

Compact displays for checkout counters and impulse purchases, maximizing visibility.

Floor Standing Display Units (FSDU)

Freestanding displays for high-traffic areas, perfect for promotions and new launches.

Shelf Ready Packaging (SRP)

Easy-to-open packaging that transitions smoothly from transit to retail shelf.

Display Shippers

Dual-purpose packaging that serves as both a shipping container and a retail display.

Key Features & Benefits

Eye-Catching Design

Vibrant graphics and innovative structures to attract customer attention.

Reinforce brand identity and messaging at the point of sale.

Easy Assembly & Setup

Detailed Specifications

Our retail display packaging is engineered to meet the demanding needs of the retail environment, combining aesthetic appeal with structural integrity. We work closely with you to develop displays that perfectly showcase your products and align with your brand strategy.

Material & Structural Design

We utilize a variety of materials and design techniques to create effective retail displays:

  • Corrugated Cardboard: Lightweight, strong, and versatile for various display types. Available in different flute sizes (e.g., E-flute, B-flute) for optimal performance.
  • Paperboard: Offers a smoother surface for high-quality printing, ideal for smaller displays and packaging components.
  • Structural Engineering: Custom designs to ensure stability, product support, and ease of interaction for consumers.
  • Sustainable Choices: Options for recycled content, FSC-certified materials, and soy/water-based inks.

Printing & Finishing Options

Make your displays pop with our advanced printing and finishing capabilities:

  • High-Quality Printing: Flexographic, offset, and digital printing for vibrant colors and sharp graphics.
  • Coatings & Laminates: Gloss, matte, UV coatings, and laminations for enhanced durability and visual appeal.
  • Special Finishes: Embossing, debossing, foil stamping, and window patching to create a premium feel.
  • Die-Cutting: Precise custom shapes and cutouts to create unique and engaging display structures.

Common Applications

Our retail display packaging is suitable for a wide array of products and industries:

  • Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG): Snacks, beverages, personal care items.
  • Electronics: Small gadgets, accessories, promotional items.
  • Cosmetics & Beauty Products: Makeup, skincare, fragrances.
  • Toys & Games: Highlighting new releases and popular items.
  • Seasonal & Promotional Campaigns: Creating excitement and driving impulse buys.
