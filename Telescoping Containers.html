Telescopic Containers

Versatile two-piece packaging solutions with adjustable heights for secure product protection and premium presentation.

Telescopic containers consist of two separate pieces - a base and a lid that slides over it. This design allows for variable height adjustment, making them ideal for products of different sizes. Their sturdy construction and elegant appearance make them perfect for premium packaging needs.

Telescopic Container Types

Full Telescope Box

Complete coverage with lid extending fully over the base for maximum protection

Partial Telescope Box

Lid covers only part of the base, offering a balance of protection and material efficiency

Rigid Telescope Box

Extra-sturdy construction for premium products requiring superior protection

Sleeve Style Telescope

Elegant design with a sleeve that slides over the base, ideal for luxury products

Detailed Specifications

Telescopic containers are versatile packaging solutions that offer superior protection and presentation for a wide range of products. Their two-piece construction consists of a base and a lid that slides over it, creating a secure enclosure that can be adjusted to accommodate products of different heights.

Material Options

Our telescopic containers are available in various materials to suit your specific requirements:

  • Standard Corrugated: Economical option with good protection for everyday products
  • Heavy-Duty Corrugated: Enhanced strength for heavier items requiring extra protection
  • Rigid Paperboard: Premium option for luxury products with superior finish and appearance
  • Eco-Friendly Materials: Sustainable options including recycled content and biodegradable coatings

Customization Options

We offer extensive customization to ensure your telescopic containers perfectly match your brand and product needs:

  • Custom Sizing: Tailored dimensions to fit your specific product requirements
  • Printing & Finishing: High-quality offset, digital, or flexographic printing with various coating options
  • Interior Fittings: Custom inserts, dividers, or cushioning to secure products in place
  • Special Features: Windows, handles, magnetic closures, or ribbon pulls for enhanced functionality

Common Applications

Telescopic containers are ideal for a wide range of products and industries:

  • Retail Products: Clothing, accessories, electronics, and specialty items
  • Food & Beverage: Gourmet foods, confectionery, and premium beverages
  • Cosmetics & Beauty: Perfumes, skincare sets, and makeup collections
  • Corporate Gifts: Executive presents, award presentations, and promotional packages
