About Us

Our Story

Founded in 2019 by visionary entrepreneur Mrs. Vijayalakshmi D, Vijaya Packaging Industries has grown from a small corrugated box manufacturing unit into one of the region's leading packaging solution providers.

With a commitment to quality, innovation, and sustainability, we have earned the trust of clients across various industries including FMCG, electronics, pharmaceuticals, and e-commerce.


Our Founder

Mrs. Vijayalakshmi's journey in the predominantly male-driven packaging industry began with a vision to create sustainable packaging solutions while empowering the local community. Her leadership has been recognized with several entrepreneurship awards, including the prestigious Business Excellence Award in 2018.

Under her guidance, Vijaya Packaging Industries has consistently pushed the boundaries of innovation while maintaining a strong focus on environmental responsibility and social impact.

Our Sustainability Commitment

Eco-Friendly By Design

At Vijaya Packaging Industries, environmental responsibility is at the core of everything we do. We believe that quality packaging and sustainability can go hand in hand.

Our comprehensive approach to sustainability encompasses our raw materials, manufacturing processes, and end-of-life recyclability.

♻️

Recycled Materials

Our corrugated board contains up to 70% post-consumer recycled content, reducing demand for virgin materials.

💧

Water-Based Inks

We exclusively use water-based inks that are free from harmful VOCs and heavy metals.

⚡

Energy Efficiency

Our facility operates with energy-efficient machinery and practices to minimize our carbon footprint.

🌱

Waste Reduction

Our zero-waste initiative ensures that 95% of our production waste is recycled or repurposed.
