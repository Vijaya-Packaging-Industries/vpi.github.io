Tri-Fold Brochure Content Plan

Use this guide to place content in your Canva design. The layout is structured panel by panel for clarity.

Outside Spread (Panels 5, 6, 1)

Panel 5: Inside Flap

Primary Image:

Use the main image from your sustainability section.

Filename: Sustainable Packaging Process.jpeg

Headline:

Our Commitment to Sustainability

Text Content (Brief bullet points):

  • Eco-Friendly by Design, Ethical by Choice
  • High Percentage of Recycled Materials
  • Exclusive Use of Water-Based Inks
  • Energy-Efficient Green Practices
  • Over 95% of Production Waste Recycled

Tip: Use icons (like a leaf or recycle symbol) next to each bullet point in Canva.
