About Vijaya Packaging

Our Story: A Legacy of Innovation & Trust

Founded in 2019 by visionary entrepreneur Mrs. Vijayalakshmi D, Vijaya Packaging Industries has rapidly evolved from a dedicated corrugated box manufacturing unit into one of the region's leading and most trusted packaging solution providers. We are a proud woman-led enterprise, deeply rooted in the vibrant city of Mysore, and committed to excellence.

Meet Our Founder: Mrs. Vijayalakshmi D

Under her inspiring guidance, Vijaya Packaging Industries consistently pushes the boundaries of innovation, all while maintaining an unwavering focus on environmental responsibility and positive social impact.

"We don't just make boxes; we build trust and deliver solutions that protect what's valuable, both for our clients and our planet." - Mrs. Vijayalakshmi D
